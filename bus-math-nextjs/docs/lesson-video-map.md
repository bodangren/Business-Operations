Checked: 2026-10-08.

This map lists the topics in all 80 lessons. The site includes 33 selected videos on 33 lesson pages. Optional Excel resources appear on 14 pages.

## Source order

1. Use [Accounting Stuff](https://www.youtube.com/@AccountingStuff) for accounting concepts.
2. Use [Accounting 101 by The Finance Storyteller](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKbmcnUUQMlnWPLx9IeS-cYec2r7GzJ3S) when the preferred channel has no suitable match. Its Gross vs Net video supports Unit 5 Lesson 2.
3. Use [Excel for accounting by Ahmed Nasser STT](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLhH2tcp0LzokPuSWqtusXcTGxvlQP2rpv) and the [Excel Accounting Playlist by excelisfun](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLrRPvpgDmw0ndqetqYG0ocKrr-3FvouJ-) for optional review.

The teacher's Excel tutorials remain the main instruction. Excel videos help students who miss class or need to review a procedure. They do not add required tasks.

## Match labels

The accounting match applies to the two accounting sources. Optional Excel matches appear in a separate column.

- **Direct (12):** The video teaches the main accounting topic.
- **Partial (24):** The video supports part of the lesson. Keep the page's complete procedure.
- **Review (16):** Reuse an earlier accounting explanation when needed.
- **No match verified (28):** No suitable accounting video was verified in the requested sources.

The last two columns show the videos added to the site. A candidate can remain in the map without a new embed. Launch and project pages use their current activities. Students can return to the earlier lesson for review.
