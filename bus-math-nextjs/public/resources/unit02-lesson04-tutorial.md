# Unit 2 Lesson 4 — Manual Month-End Close

Use `BM U02L04 - Textbook Aligned.xlsx` from OneDrive. The website copy is `unit02-lesson04-student.xlsx`.

This worksheet is a separate March practice case. Use its opening balances and month-end facts. The on-page challenge has different values.

Calculate the amounts by hand. Enter your results in the worksheet or on paper. You do not need to build formulas or named ranges. The completed reference uses formulas to show the links between the steps.

## 1. Check the opening trial balance

Open **Unadjusted TB**. Add the debit balances and the credit balances. Both totals must be $51,700. Correct an opening difference before you record adjustments.

Read the facts in rows 24–30. These facts support six adjustments:

- Supplies used: opening Supplies minus supplies on hand.
- Insurance expired: policy cost divided by policy length, multiplied by months used.
- Depreciation: the March amount given in the worksheet.
- Accrued wages: wages earned but not paid.
- Earned revenue: advance service now complete.
- Accrued revenue: completed work not yet billed.

## 2. Record the adjustments

Open **Adjustments**. Use one debit line and one credit line for each fact. Enter the account name and amount. Enter 0 on the unused side of each line.

Add the debit and credit columns. Check the accounts as well as the total. A balanced journal can still contain a wrong account or amount.

## 3. Prepare the adjusted trial balance

Open **Adjusted TB**. For each account:

1. Start with its unadjusted debit balance minus its unadjusted credit balance.
2. Add its adjustment debits.
3. Subtract its adjustment credits.
4. Put a positive result in After Dr. Put the absolute value of a negative result in After Cr.
5. Enter 0 on the unused side. Enter 0 for an account with no adjustment.

Check that total adjusted debits equal total adjusted credits.

## 4. Prepare the financial statements

Open **Financial Statements**. Complete the Income Statement first. Subtract total expenses from Service Revenue to get Net Income.

Add Net Income to beginning Retained Earnings. This case has no dividends.

Complete the Balance Sheet. Subtract Accumulated Depreciation from the asset total. Check that Assets equal Liabilities plus ending Retained Earnings.

## 5. Record the closing entries

Open **Closing Entries**. Use the course Income Summary method:

1. Debit Service Revenue for its adjusted balance. Credit Income Summary.
2. Debit Income Summary for total expenses. Credit each expense account for its adjusted balance.
3. For this profit case, debit Income Summary for Net Income. Credit Retained Earnings.

Enter 0 on each unused debit or credit side. Check that revenue, expenses, and Income Summary have zero balances after closing.

## 6. Prepare the post-closing trial balance

Open **Post-closing TB**. Carry forward the permanent account balances. Use ending Retained Earnings. Leave revenue, expense accounts, and Income Summary out of this trial balance.

Enter 0 on each unused side. Add the debit and credit columns. They must be equal.
